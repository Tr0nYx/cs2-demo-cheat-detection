<threat_model>
Trust boundaries:
- Host filesystem to containers through bind mounts.
- Developer `.env` values into container environment.
- Container network between app services, Redis, and PostgreSQL.

STRIDE register:
- T-01 Spoofing: Services could use mismatched credentials if `.env.example` and Compose drift. Mitigation: centralize service credentials in `.env.example` and reference them from Compose.
- T-02 Information disclosure: Demo files or `.env` could be committed. Mitigation: `.gitignore` and `.dockerignore` must include `.env` and `*.dem`.
- T-03 Elevation of privilege: App containers could run as root. Mitigation is completed in Plans 02 and 03 with non-root runtime users.
</threat_model>
