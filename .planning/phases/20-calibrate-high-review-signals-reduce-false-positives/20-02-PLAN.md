# 20-02-PLAN: Feature Evidence Gates and Conservative Caps

<objective>
Apply player-specific evidence gates and conservative score caps to the existing feature extractors so weak proxies, low sample counts, unknown weapon data, and parser gaps cannot create high review feature scores.
</objective>

<tasks>
<task>
  <id>20-02-01</id>
  <title>Gate aimbot scores by repeated suspicious kill-window evidence</title>
  <type>implementation</type>
  <files>
    - python/features/aimbot.py
    - tests/test_features_aimbot.py
  </files>
  <action>
    Replace max-subscore dominance with evidence counting. High aimbot scores must require multiple suspicious kill windows and at least two independent aim signals where possible, such as snap and jerk. Single-window or single-signal cases should be capped and annotated with low/medium confidence metadata.
  </action>
  <acceptance_criteria>
    - Single extreme snap measurement cannot produce an uncapped high score.
    - Multiple suspicious kill windows can still produce strong evidence.
    - Metadata includes sample count, independent signals, confidence, evidence strength, and cap reason when capped.
  </acceptance_criteria>
  <verify>
    $env:PYTHONPATH='python'; pytest tests/test_features_aimbot.py -q
  </verify>
</task>

    - Low peek counts cap the score and emit warnings.

<threat_model>

Mitigation: use the shared metadata contract from Plan 20-01 and add extractor-specific tests for cap reasons.

Mitigation: preserve raw measurements and independent signal lists so gates can evolve without losing observability.
</threat_model>
